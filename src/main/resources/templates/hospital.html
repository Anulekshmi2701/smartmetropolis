    Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences & Technology

    SCTIMST is a world-renowned institution for cardiac and neurological treatments. It combines cutting-edge research with excellent patient care. SCTIMST is also known for its pioneering work in biomedical technology.

    Location: Poojappura, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695012

    Contact Email: director@sctimst.ac.in

    Regional Cancer Centre (RCC)

    RCC Thiruvananthapuram is one of the top cancer care facilities in South India. Established in 1981, it offers advanced cancer treatment, education, and research. The hospital is known for its affordable treatment and various outreach programs..

    Location: Medical College Campus, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695011

    Contact Email: info@rcctvm.gov.in

    General Hospital, Thiruvananthapuram

    Established in 1864, the General Hospital in Thiruvananthapuram is one of the oldest hospitals in Kerala. It provides basic and specialized health care services at an affordable cost.

    Location: General Hospital Road, Pulimoodu, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695035

    Contact Email: gh.tvm@kerala.gov.in

    Women and Children Hospital, Thycaudm

    This is one of the key healthcare centers in Thiruvananthapuram for women and children. The hospital is well known for its maternity and pediatric care.

    Location: Thycaud, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695014

    Contact Email: thycaudhospital@kerala.gov.ins

    Mental Health Center, Peroorkada

    The Mental Health Center in Peroorkada is one of the leading psychiatric hospitals in Kerala, providing specialized care for mental health issues. The hospital offers various services, including outpatient care, inpatient care, rehabilitation, and counseling services for individuals with mental health conditions. It has a long history of serving the community and plays a crucial role in mental health awareness and treatment in the region.

    Location: Peroorkada, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695005

    Contact Email: mhctvm@gmail.com

    KIMS Health, Trivandrum
