    Explore the Best Cinematic Experiences

    Discover the top movie theaters in Trivandrum offering high-quality cinematic experiences. Whether you're looking for historic theaters or modern multiplexes, find the right spot to enjoy your favorite movies!

    Aries Plex SL Cinemas

    4K digital multiplex with luxurious seating, multiple screens, and advanced sound systems. A pioneer in modern cinematic technology.

    Location: MG Road, Near Ayurveda College, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 233 2002

    New Theatre

    An iconic single-screen theater that blends history and modernity, offering a unique movie experience.

    Location: Statue Junction, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 247 3776

    Carnival Cinemas

    A modern multiplex located in the Mall of Travancore, offering multiple screens and top-notch facilities.

    Location: Mall of Travancore, NH Bypass, Eanchakkal, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 669 9999

    Kalabhavan Theatre

    A cultural hub for art films and independent cinema. Perfect for those seeking an artistic movie experience.

    Location: Vazhuthacaud, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 232 2123

    Kripa Cinemas

    Known for its excellent sound systems and comfortable seating, this theater screens regional and international films.

    Location: Overbridge, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 232 2344

    Sree Padmanabha Theatre

    A family-friendly theater known for its proximity to Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple and comfortable viewing experience.

    Location: East Fort, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 247 3830

    Nila Theatre

    An affordable theater that offers good screen quality and primarily features regional films.

    Location: Thampanoor, Trivandrum

    Contact: +91 471 233 1864

    Kairali-Sree Theatre

    A dual-screen cinema complex offering both classic and latest movie experiences in Trivandrum.
